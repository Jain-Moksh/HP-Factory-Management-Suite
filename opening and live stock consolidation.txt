## ISSUE DESCRIPTION

There is a stock inconsistency issue in the Stock Master / Item Master section.

Problem:
When updating the OPENING STOCK value of an item, the LIVE STOCK (`items.stock`) is not updating accordingly.

Expected Behavior:
If opening stock changes, live stock should automatically adjust based on the difference between:

* previous opening stock
* new opening stock

Example:

* Existing:

  * opening stock = 100
  * live stock = 250

* User updates opening stock to 130

* Difference = +30

Expected result:

* live stock should become 280

Similarly:

* Existing:

  * opening stock = 100
  * live stock = 250

* User updates opening stock to 70

* Difference = -30

Expected result:

* live stock should become 220

---

## IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULE

Opening stock is part of the live stock source of truth.

Therefore:

* Updating opening stock must proportionally update current stock.
* Only the DIFFERENCE should be applied.
* Existing billing/purchase transactions must remain untouched.

Formula:
new_live_stock = current_live_stock + (new_open_stock - old_open_stock)
